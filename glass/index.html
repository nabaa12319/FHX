Import from Blender

Export from Blender as .glb (File › Export › glTF 2.0, format "glTF Binary") or .obj. Each object becomes its own layer; choose a glass type for each one or for all at once.

Glass cutting table

Pick a stock sheet, plan with a marker, cut it straight, at an angle or by hand. Every piece moves on its own and gets its own decision: add to model, keep, or waste.

Notes

A note in space is pinned to a spot in the gallery: click it in the list to fly there. A general note lives only in this list. Notes hold text, a drawing and photos to collect ideas.

Overall light

Light sources

Lights are listed under Layers. The first 4 spots cast real shadows. Glass reflects and refracts with an index of 1.52.

Epson projectors (up to 10)

Projectors are listed under Layers. Each one takes its own image or MP4. The image passes through clear glass (faint trace on it), bounces off mirrors and lands clearly on frosted glass and walls. Image size comes from throw ratio × real distance.
